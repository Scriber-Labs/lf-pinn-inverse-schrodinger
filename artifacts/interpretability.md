# 🧠 Interpretability Axis

## 💡Ideas:
For the nondimensionalized quantum harmonic oscillator (QHO), eigenstates are eigenfunctions of:

$$ H = -\frac{1}{2}\frac{\partial^2}{
\partial x^2}+ \frac{1}{2}x^2$$
